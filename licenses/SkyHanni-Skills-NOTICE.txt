including the normal skill leveling curve and extrapolated post-60 overflow slope.

https://github.com/SkyHanniStudios/SkyHanni/tree/beta/src/main/java/at/hannibal2/skyhanni/features/skillprogress

Its source is included in the sources JAR and available at
https://github.com/johnceoskyblock/sparkling-mutuals.

https://api.hypixel.net/v2/resources/skyblock/skills
