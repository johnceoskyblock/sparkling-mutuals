SHARD SALE GUIDANCE — DESIGN FOR APPROVAL
Date: 2026-11-03
Status: proposed; no implementation or publishing approved by this document.

INTENT AND DECISIONS
Help the player decide whether to list their Safari shards now or wait briefly. Show the decision directly in the Hunting Box, and warn when previously observed sell offers become unattractive. The user wants automatically calculated targets using about a week of history, with a selling horizon of a few hours because prices fluctuate frequently. The proposed default horizon is three hours; this number is the designer's choice, not a user-specified deadline. A target is an advisory competitive sell-offer price, not an instant-sell price, guaranteed profit, price forecast or promise that an order will fill within three hours.

APPROACHES CONSIDERED
1. Hourly Google Doc targets plus live quotes: simple and matches the user's existing process, but target judgments can remain stale during rapid changes and require the other chat to maintain the document.
2. Automatic recent-market targets with rolling-week context: chosen direction in the conversation. Requires historical data, validation and explicit unknown states, but removes the manual hourly dependency and can react within minutes. Use official Hypixel current quotes and a public historical provider, provisionally Coflnet.
3. Locally collected history only: no historical-provider dependency, but needs a week of continuous collection and cannot reconstruct hours while the game is closed. Not suitable as the initial primary source.
The Google Doc is reference material only in this increment; no silent fallback to its older judgments and no edits to it or its automation.

SCOPE AND ANTI-GOALS
Support the 37 recognized Safari shard types wherever market history is adequate. Fetch historical data lazily for visible Hunting Box shards and shards in observed personal sell offers, rather than all products on every refresh. This all-species scope is a designer proposal; /profit's top-ten ranking remains unchanged and separate. Exclude Safari Essence and unrelated items in this increment.
Add one opt-in native setting, Shard sale guidance, default off. While enabled in SkyBlock: show slot highlights and optional tooltip explanations; observe orders and issue transition warnings. Turning it off removes highlights and stops requests/alerts. Do not resurrect the shelved Hunting Box/chest valuation, change server items, automate purchases/orders/cancellations, require personal API credentials, or push changes to GitHub.

DATA AND FRESHNESS
One shared asynchronous public Hypixel Bazaar request at most once per minute while guidance is enabled and relevant gameplay is active. Retain the existing slower behavior when no fast consumer is active. No request from rendering or per slot. Current competitive ask is the lowest validated positive price in buy_summary; bids come from sell_summary. Check official lastUpdated, finite positive values and a coherent spread. Do not mistake listed depth or moving-week volume for executed interval trades.
Use approximately 168 hours of historical prices for context plus the latest day at finer resolution. Weekly and daily public Coflnet endpoints were tested for Hideonwall, returning 82 and 480 samples respectively. This is evidence for one product, not certification of all 37. Before shipping, verify field meaning, timestamp timezone, availability, limits and usage terms. If those cannot be established, stop short of enabling automatic advice and report the limitation; do not invent timestamps or silently substitute chart scraping.
Refresh finer history approximately every ten minutes for watched products, week history hourly, with a serialized rate-limited queue, bounded retries, Retry-After handling and a hard memory/disk bound. Limit to 37 recognized IDs, seven days of binned samples plus a small validation margin; prune on load and refresh. Reject malformed, future and duplicate samples. Store data source and observation time. Current quotes older than three minutes, missing recent history, inadequate coverage, inconsistent prices or failed provider validation produce UNKNOWN, with no price-change alerts. The builder may tighten coverage thresholds after inspecting actual supported products; requirements must be explicit and tested, not silently relaxed to show a color.

TARGET METHOD — PROPOSED HEURISTIC, TO VALIDATE
Use sell-offer asks, in coins per shard before tax, consistently across live/history data. Normalize fine history to closed five-minute bins. Weekly coarse data supplies context only; never interpolate two-hour weekly samples into fictitious five-minute observations.
Recalculate from available data at most every five minutes, using cached history and fresh quotes. Initial candidate: a robust upper-middle recent price, blending 60% of the last three-hour 60th percentile, 30% of the last 24-hour 60th percentile, and 10% of the week's 60th percentile. Clamp the result to the 10th–90th percentile range of the last six hours so an old weekly peak cannot dictate the target. Define percentiles on time bins, not number of provider observations. Isolated transient spikes must not dominate; sustained new levels must remain eligible rather than being filtered as outliers forever.
Use robust 15-minute medians to identify a sustained falling regime: three consecutive closed medians falling, with at least a 1% total decline. In that regime, lower the candidate toward the most recent 30-minute median and allow SELL at a fresh competitive ask rather than perpetually waiting for recovery. Label the explanation as falling-market exit guidance. This is a designer's proposed rule, not evidence of an optimal strategy.
Show SELL when a fresh ask reaches the target within a narrow tolerance; show HOLD below it when no falling-market exit applies. Suggested initial tolerance/hysteresis: enter SELL at 99.5% of the target; change SELL to HOLD only below 98.5%, confirmed by two fresh observations at least 60 seconds apart. Repeated reads of one server snapshot do not count as confirmation. UNKNOWN supersedes any prior color immediately when required freshness or coverage fails. Reacquiring data establishes a new state and must not fabricate a price-drop transition.
The exact quantile, blending, trend and band constants are builder-tunable through historical replay before acceptance, without changing the intended three-hour advisory horizon. Report the selected values and their observed tradeoffs; do not claim the initial heuristic is validated. Replay rolling decisions with only then-available data, compare against a simple competitive-list-now baseline and a recent-only rule, and report three-hour target-touch rates, waiting time, missed downside and data coverage. Target touches are not order fills. Color eligibility is conservative and product-specific. Designer-proposed initial acceptance rules: at least 20 complete, non-overlapping three-hour replay windows spanning at least four distinct days for that product; at least 60% of HOLD windows touch the then-fixed target within three hours; median time to touch among successful windows no longer than 90 minutes; and no more than 10% of HOLD windows finish more than 5% below their starting ask. Freeze each replay target at the decision time for these measurements, and report changes from later target updates separately. Reserve the most recent full day as a chronological holdout; tune only on earlier data, and apply the touch/downside rules on both sets where at least five eligible HOLD decisions exist in each. These are proposed product acceptance choices, not statistically established guarantees. A product with inadequate samples, poor results or inconclusive holdout remains UNKNOWN/gray, with both SELL/HOLD colors and market-change alerts withheld; an experimental label does not bypass this rule. Correctness tests alone do not qualify a price model. Report coverage and failures before delivery. If no products qualify, report that the requested automatic guidance cannot yet be delivered reliably rather than declaring a neutral-only feature complete. Changes to these acceptance rules require review, not silent builder relaxation.

PERSONAL SELL-OFFER WARNINGS
The public Bazaar API does not expose personal orders. Observe the player's Manage Orders/order-detail GUI, with narrowly validated shard identity, order side and available price/quantity/status evidence. Do not assume incomplete pagination is a complete inventory of orders, or that an unseen order was cancelled. Use explicit observed completion/cancellation where available. Associate watches with account and confirmed SkyBlock profile; if profile identity is unavailable, suppress tracking rather than combine profiles.
Only freshly observed active offers qualify for a direct active-order statement. Watches persist locally with last-seen time for continuity, but need revalidation after restart/profile changes. For offscreen offers, warnings must say Last observed sell offer for [shard] ... price guidance changed to HOLD; check your orders and include age, because it may already have filled. If fresh advice switches SELL to confirmed HOLD, send one local chat advisory per product transition, grouping multiple offers rather than claiming one-to-one identity without evidence. A newly observed offer already in HOLD may get one clearly labeled advisory, not a fabricated drop report. Re-alert only after a confirmed return to SELL and another confirmed decline; use a per-product cooldown and do not spam on each poll. Suppress alerts for UNKNOWN and during profile/account transitions. No remote notifications when the game is closed, no inventory/order uploads and no automatic trading actions.

VALIDATION AND ACCEPTANCE
Meaningful unit coverage: identity/slot parsing, exact bid/ask semantics, sorting/deduplication/timezones, staleness, partial coverage, rolling-window bounds, isolated spikes versus sustained regimes, hysteresis with distinct observations, rate limits/cancellation, profile isolation and order pagination/lifecycle/deduplication. Historical replay must be chronological with no lookahead and report limitations rather than equating touched prices with actual fills.
Build and run the relevant existing checks for all three supported targets. Exercise native menus/rendering and settings on each available client; record any untested live paths explicitly. Actual Hypixel Manage Orders layouts, Hunting Box display and capture of filled/cancelled orders need safe gameplay evidence; do not fabricate a successful live test. No automatic account interaction to place test orders.
Success: reliable data shows stable, explainable colors tied to a few-hour advisory decision; unavailable data stays neutral; verified transitions produce one clear local notification; ordinary /profit behavior and shelved-feature boundaries remain intact.
