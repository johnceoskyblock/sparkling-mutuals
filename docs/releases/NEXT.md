## What’s New in 4.4.5

### All editions

- Optional Unique-mode checks turn off once when you update. Enable the checks your party requests under **Safari → Modes → Sparkling Checks Options**. The **Biome complete** banner and automatic biome reports (`fd`, `hd`, `cd`) follow your selected checks. Sparkling mode uses its usual checks.
- In Unique mode, `/rates` counts one check per disabled manual-check critter. Gemzie always uses its full spawn average.
- Fixed flickering Forest and Haunted progress and objective displays while holding starting food or coins with **Announce starting quest items** off.
- Rank biomes under **Party → Interactions → Biome Hotspot Preferences**. Supported party sync servers use the ranking to break equal-win ties between players claiming the same hotspot.
- The **Hotspots** HUD keeps counting when the same party resumes after a break. The total includes the current run once your Safari admission is confirmed.
- Sparkling catch announcements show the recorded species count, including `#1 (NEW!)` for a first discovery.
- Removed the automatic follow-up naming teammates who still owe a ticket confirmation.

### Standard

- Recognize Sparkling critters from visible skins and corroborated particles. A started alert stays visible for its normal duration.
- Keep partially visible critters marked with a depth-tested box when their whole-model glow cannot be shown safely. Apply body visibility to hidden species and account for Duplico’s disguise.
- Removed the Hideyho inside/outside location banner.

### Full and Full No Auto Update

- **Hideyho location** turns on once with this update. To change it afterward, go to **Safari → Banner → Encounter alerts → Hideyho location**.
- Sparkle Warping gives teammates up to two seconds to finish an island transfer before the entry window closes. The Reparty and Warp sliders still set the baseline timing.

### Full with Auto Update

- **Auto Update** turns on once the first time you launch 4.4.5. You can turn it off in the update screen, and your choice is saved.

## Downloads

GitHub releases provide all three builds for Minecraft **26.1.2**, **26.2**, and **26.3**. Install one JAR with the matching `+mc` suffix.

| Edition | Filename suffix before `+mc` | Updates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | None | Manual downloads |
| **Full** | `-full` | Verified in-game downloads with a configurable Auto Update toggle |
| **Full No Auto Update** | `-full-no-auto-update` | Manual downloads |
