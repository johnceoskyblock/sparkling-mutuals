# Macaw

[Index](README.md) · [Shared evidence rules](TRACKING.md)

**Biome:** Forest. **Recorded Full Clear baseline:** Variable; determine from actual spawns. Baseline counts are carried from earlier notes, not newly verified server guarantees.

## Evidence

- **Legendary: can escape a standard capsule; Masterful catches are guaranteed once hit.** An escaped critter may return with a replacement UUID; that alone does not prove an additional Macaw spawn.
- Your capture confirmation adds one personal and one shared catch. A party loot-share confirmation adds one shared catch, attributed to its catcher.
- Keep any captures received before visiting Forest; subtract them from the remaining requirement once, as described in the shared rules.

## Completion

| Mode | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Unique | One shared catch; if anyone still needs this sparkling variant, also satisfy the species' Sparkling evidence. The missing-species row may disappear after the catch. Proven no-spawn remains an unavailable outcome. |
| Full Clear | Resolve all known food use, then one confirmed shared Macaw catch completes Macaw. Additional scanned/live Macaws do not block this species or Forest Full Clear. |
| Sparkling | Resolve the shared feeder opportunities and check the resulting birds; one catch does not resolve unused food. |

## Specific deductions

- Mid-run entry can recover missing feeding history with no remaining food-source markers or held food and at least nine accounted-for feeding opportunities (shared catches + current live uncaptured birds, paired Macaws), plus known extras, inside the Forest central scan radius. Preserve each mode's catch rules; see [Forest mid-run](TRACKING.md#forest-mid-run-birds).

- Forest highlights may select predicted Wriggleworm/Yogi Berry/Bag of Seeds drops via the [floor-drop predictor](FLOOR_DROPS.md). A prediction is not food collected or used; all shared-feeder checks remain unchanged.
- After a party unused-food notice, keep Forest's extra-food gate pending until that food is used and additional birds are evidenced by spawn messages or new shared-capture + current-scan accounting. Reusing the previous bird total is insufficient. Full Clear must resolve the additional food/spawn evidence; its one-Macaw capture exception still applies.
- If any bird discovery is needed, a player finishing another biome with unused starting bird food outside Forest receives a local alert and sends a party notice. That notice reopens the relevant bird/feeding checks; no reminders if all bird discoveries are owned. See [starting-food reminders](TRACKING.md#unused-starting-foodcoin-reminders).
- Full Clear exception: one own capture or party loot share is enough after all known starting/floor/party-reported food has been used. Remaining Macaws do not block Forest completion or its Full Clear message. A personal Full Clear PB still needs a personal Macaw catch. Reopen feeding work for newly known unused food, then apply the same one-catch exception after it is used.
- A matching birdfeeder announcement proves a spawn and a food use. Record its species and stated quantity; Two Macaws means two critters but one feeding.
- Count personal starting food + Forest pickups as minimum known food. Teammates may collect food or stack their own starting food in the feeder; accept announcements beyond the personal food total.
- Track resulting UUIDs separately from feeding events. One spawn message followed by its UUID is one spawn, not two.
- Use shared catches + currently scanned uncaptured Macaw UUIDs to account for this species, without counting its spawn message or escape replacement again. Correlate all bird species with the known food demand.
- Capture-GUI color: keep this row red while known food remains unused/queued. Once food use is resolved and one shared Macaw catch is recorded, turn it green even if other Macaws remain in the scan; other bird species need not be complete. Reevaluate on new food/spawns. See [shared feeder rules](TRACKING.md#forest-birds-shared-feeder-independent-completion).
- No-Macaw evidence needs every feeding opportunity resolved with no Macaw spawn/sighting. It marks unavailable, not a Macaw catch for Unique biome completion; party `fd` cannot advance Unique progress. A bonus Rainbow Feather message is a reward, not another Macaw capture.
- Reported in Sparkling testing: a Macaw escaped a capsule, reappeared and regained ESP. A replacement UUID may explain this; it is not another feeder spawn, successful catch or automatically new discovery opportunity. See [escape identity](TRACKING.md#capsule-escape-and-replacement-identity).

A locally resolved source check survives biome reentry and unloading for this run. Unknown scans do not revoke it; new food/coins, visible unresolved sources or uncaught Sparklings still block completion. See [retained checks](TRACKING.md).
